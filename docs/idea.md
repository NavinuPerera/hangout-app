\# Hangout App — Initial Idea



\## Overview



Hangout App is a web application designed to help groups of friends organize hangouts and manage the money involved in those events.



The main idea is to combine event planning, attendance tracking, expense splitting, personal balances, and group funding in one application.



\## Events



Users can create events or hangouts and invite their friends or members of a friend group.



An event could contain information such as:



\- Event name

\- Date and time

\- Location

\- Description

\- Invited friends



Invited users can respond to the event to indicate whether they are attending, not attending, or are unsure.



This allows everyone in the group to easily see who is planning to attend.



\## Shared Expenses



After or during an event, users can record expenses.



For example, if one friend pays the entire restaurant bill for the group, the expense can be added to the event and divided among the people who participated.



The application will keep track of:



\- Who paid

\- How much was paid

\- Who participated in the expense

\- How much each person owes



Expenses may eventually support different ways of splitting the cost, such as equal splits or custom amounts.



\## Personal Balance Dashboard



Each user will have a personal dashboard showing their current balances with their friends.



For example:



\- You owe Andrew $30

\- Ketan owes you $15

\- Satya owes you $20



Balances can come from multiple previous events instead of requiring users to settle every expense immediately.



Users will eventually be able to settle their balances and record that a debt has been paid.



\## Group Pots



Users can create shared money pots for group purchases.



For example, if one member of a friend group has a birthday coming up, the other members could create a pot and contribute money toward buying that person a gift.



Group pots could also be used for things such as:



\- Birthday gifts

\- Trips

\- Airbnb deposits

\- Tickets

\- Shared purchases

\- Other group activities



A pot may also have the option to be hidden from a particular group member when it is being used for a surprise.



\## Payments



The application will initially focus on tracking expenses and balances rather than directly processing real money.



In the future, payment functionality could be added so users can settle balances or contribute to pots directly through the application.



Apple Pay is one payment method that could eventually be supported.



\## Initial Goal



The first version of the application should focus on the core experience:



1\. Creating accounts and friend groups

2\. Creating events

3\. Inviting friends and tracking attendance

4\. Recording shared expenses

5\. Calculating who owes whom

6\. Displaying balances on a personal dashboard

7\. Settling balances

8\. Creating and contributing to group pots



Additional features can be added after the core system is working.
